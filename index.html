Who We Are

Malaysia's Leading
NVOCC Partner

AK Best Ventures is an international freight forwarding company established in Malaysia in 2016 — recognized as one of the most reliable and competitive NVOCC players in the market providing total logistics services.

01Non Vessel Operating Common Carrier (NVOCC) specializing in total cargo management — sea, air, and domestic freight forwarding, Customs Clearance, and Cargo Consolidation.
02Full services: Breakbulk, Warehousing, Project Cargo Handling, and Cross Border Transportation across Malaysia and internationally.
03Growing leader in Domestic Logistics, Container Haulage, and B2C Logistics — with projects running throughout the world on an ongoing basis.
